# Krish Gupta — krishg.com

**Register:** brand (portfolio is the product)

## Purpose

A personal portfolio for builders, collaborators, and investors who want the person behind Reseter.css, community scale (HTM, PRUMUN), and Ciel. The homepage leads with selected work; `/work` is the full index with short case studies per project.

## Audience

- Engineers evaluating OSS and taste
- Community organizers and hackathon peers
- Founders and investors doing a quick credibility scan
- Future teammates at Ciel or Christ University projects

## Positioning

Software engineer, poet, and founder at seventeen. Through-line: **systems for humans**, now **systems for agents**. Voice is literate and precise, not hype. "Perfectionist overkill engineer & poet" is the anchor line.

## Strategic principles

1. **Content is the interface** — minimal chrome; warm paper, one typeface (Zodiak)
2. **Motion explains hierarchy** — never decorates for its own sake
3. **Constraint as feature** — same instinct as sub-512KB blogs and jam games
4. **Honest stubs** — Work/Writing/About exist but defer depth until case studies ship

## Anti-references

- SaaS purple gradients, glassmorphism heroes, metric-card grids
- Dark-mode-by-default "devtools" aesthetic
- Award-site WebGL noise on a personal bio
- Generic AI portfolio (Inter, identical card grids, gradient text)
- Copying Ciel's gold palette or triptych on krishg.com
