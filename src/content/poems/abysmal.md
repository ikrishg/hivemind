Ascending the stairway,
Or was it a ladder?
A bar broke
And down I went
On the way I see all these people
Who were they?
Colleagues, friends, acquaintances.
Were they?
The path led towards
a bed of arrows
At least there's one for the head
Lifting it up
Saving my face from the people above
Irony is the head looking down on itself
