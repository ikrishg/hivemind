I don't need reminders of how you don't feel the same.
Oh the freezing stain, heart on blood,
This soul cannot help but go insane.
When I talk to you, my words just don't go through.
The world turns pinkish blue, stuck in the things you do.
Oh, how I wish to wake up one day,
Listening just to the things you say,
Live the world, where you have all the sway
Live the one where you perhaps could stay.
