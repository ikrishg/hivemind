Woke up in a dream
I had lost myself, my handy bag.
Losing things here might be the theme
I go looking for that bag
But why to the dreaded hall of doom?
Click, the venue swaps
The hall is full of people I know?
Some people who went down different paths
Ages ago, that too.
Then I see they're giving a test
But why not me? I jest
I notice someone I shouldn't have in that time
Walking beside a stranger I dearly know
Why so silent?
She whimsically pulls a loop on my bag
Then she runs away to the corner beneath a tree?
The bag disappears again, but to her I flee
I get there, and this other demoness taunts me
"You've done it again"
"You've distanced yet another person again"
I look again, hoping she'd say something
Horses behind in a different field, rear and neigh
What are they even doing here? In Delhi?
And then I woke up
