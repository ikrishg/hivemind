Even if darling I were in hell,
I'd threaten well, satan in his hotel,
Get him to hand me a working cell.
Because my day ends only when to you, I tell.
To the satan cell, I send fire bright,
Oh! computer, send her this byte,
To her, the packets must arrive.
And, it's already good nite.
Send this before she dims the light.
