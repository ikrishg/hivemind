In the fall, the willow wilts.
Dried, the leaves fall down,
Just as the season tilts.
Soaring leaves, through the town.
I yearn the beautiful tale,
You left with the tree behind.
Tell me leaves, before you sail.
What is the beautiful tale you left behind?
