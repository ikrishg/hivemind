a card, no longer in use,
one with an edge worn off, round,
and a white crease across it.
smudged between a book or two.
c programming and sylvia's poetry,
once a week, the book's out of the bag,
and there comes the light.
pages flip, the card is noticed,
tossed into a pile of sketch pens,
in the drawer of a man who doesn't draw.
