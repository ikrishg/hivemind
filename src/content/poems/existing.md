Yes, I know,
We had a fallout,
Perhaps the fault
It was all mine
I was wrong, I know.
You don't need to ignore me
It's starting to hurt
More than I can carry
I was a fool, I know…
But can you please stop pretending
That I don't exist…
That I don't exist.
