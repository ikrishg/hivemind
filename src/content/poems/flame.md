To live is to be a flame.
All you need is wooden canes.
What use is money and fame?
My heart exclaims.
To love is to be a flame.
Flickering in a blizzard untamed.
Standing against the icicles of society,
In unison with thy dame.
My heart exclaimed.
In love, to not be loved back,
Is to be a glass candle.
Crackle, a withering flame.
To not be loved back by thy dame.
